# Mandarin Session — 2026-09-27

Session began the evening of 2026-09-26 and continued after midnight.

## Drill format
Single Hanzi sentence → learner translates to English → correction + breakdown + pinyin.

## Strong
- 他晚上常常看电视。 → correctly translated as “He often watches TV in the evening.”
- 她每天坐公交车去上班。 → correctly translated as “She rides the bus to work every day.”
- 我姐姐每天晚上都看书。 → correctly translated as “My older sister reads every night.”
- 昨天下午我去朋友家喝茶。 → correctly translated as “Yesterday afternoon, I went to a friend’s house to drink tea.”
- Strong recognition of 常常, 晚上, 看电视, 公交车, 坐, 姐姐, 看书, 昨天下午, 喝茶.
- Learner noticed the shared character 晚 in 晚上 and 晚饭.

## Shaky / review later
- 每天 — initially missed habitual meaning; recognized correctly later.
- 妹妹 — unknown.
- 辣 — unknown.
- 朋友 — missed once, then recognized correctly later.
- 没有 — confused 没有上班 with “didn’t have to work.”
- 周末 — omitted twice.
- 散步 — confused with 跑步.
- 想 — missed “want to / would like to” nuance.
- 有时候 — unknown.
- 健身房 — unknown.
- 通常 — unknown.
- 在家 — not recognized in context.
- 做饭 — unknown.
- 星期天 — omitted.
- 晚饭 — omitted, though 晚 was then recognized as a familiar character.

## Grammar / structure
- Core sentence order is generally intact.
- Review 没有 + verb versus 不用 + verb.
- Review 想 + verb as desire/intention rather than a simple future marker.
- Review adjective + 的 + noun using concrete examples.
- Continue varied practice with time/frequency expressions; do not over-drill 常常, which is already strong.

## Next session
Mix a few of the shaky items into otherwise easy sentences, especially 周末, 有时候, 通常, 晚饭, 散步, 健身房, 妹妹, and 辣. Include one contrast for 没有 / 不用 and one for 想. Keep mastered vocabulary in incidental use rather than explicit repetition.
