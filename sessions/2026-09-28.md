# 2026-09-28

## Practiced
- Single-sentence Hanzi-to-English translation.
- Frequency/time expressions: 有时候, 通常, 常常, 周末, 星期天.
- Negation/necessity contrast: 没有 + verb vs 不用 + verb.
- 想 / 不想 for desire or intention.
- Daily-routine vocabulary involving home, work, shopping, meals, parks, and exercise.

## Strong
- 常常 was consistently recognized as "often."
- 想 / 不想 was correctly understood as want to / don't want to in repeated examples.
- 周末 improved substantially and was recognized correctly across several sentences.
- Familiar daily-routine chunks such as 在家, 下班以后, 去朋友家, 看书, 喝茶, and 去超市 were generally handled well.
- 但是 was recognized correctly in contrast sentences.

## Shaky
- 有时候 and 通常 are improving but were still occasionally swapped.
- 没有 + verb vs 不用 + verb remains shaky, especially with 上班:
  - 没有上班 = did not work / did not go to work.
  - 不用上班 = do not have to work.
- 星期天 was initially unknown once, then recognized correctly several times.
- 所以 was read as "then" instead of "so / therefore."

## New
- 不用 = do not need to / do not have to.
- 所以 = so / therefore.
- 公园 = park.
- 跑步 = run / jog.
- 但是 = but / however.

## Observations
- The learner explicitly finds character-by-character literal breakdowns useful for vocabulary memory.
- The contrast 有时候 = "there are times..." / sometimes, 通常 = generally/usually, 常常 = regularly/often was helpful.
- All Hanzi should be displayed in noticeably larger heading-sized text for readability.

## Next session
- Revisit 有时候 vs 通常 after some spacing rather than drilling them continuously.
- Re-test 没有 vs 不用 with verbs other than 上班 as well as one 上班 example.
- Recycle 星期天 and 所以.
- Keep 常常 and 想 mostly incidental unless they become shaky again.
