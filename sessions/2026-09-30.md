# 2026-09-30

## Practiced
- Continued single-sentence Hanzi-to-English translation across 2026-09-29 and 2026-09-30.
- Full weekday set 星期一–星期天 in ordinary daily-routine sentences.
- 没有 + verb vs 不用 + verb.
- Frequency expressions 有时候, 通常, 常常.
- Connectors 所以 and 但是.
- 想 / 不想 with present/current intention.
- Daily-routine vocabulary involving work, home, gym, park, supermarket, friends, cooking, dinner, and reading.

## Strong
- Core sentence order remains strong.
- Weekdays are mostly recognized correctly in context.
- 没有 vs 不用 improved substantially; the semantic distinction is usually correct now.
- 所以 is now repeatedly recognized as "so / therefore."
- 有时候 / 通常 / 常常 are much more stable than in the prior session.
- Familiar place/activity chunks such as 健身房, 公园, 超市, 在家, 做饭, 吃晚饭, 去朋友家, and 下班以后 are being parsed reliably.

## Shaky
- Time reference from context: 不想 / 不用 were occasionally translated in past tense even when there was no past-time cue.
- 星期五 vs 星期六 had one mix-up; continue incidental spaced exposure.
- 但是 was once rendered as "so" rather than "but."
- 通常 was translated as "sometimes" once at the beginning of the session, then was correct repeatedly.

## Learner preference clarified
- Slightly awkward/literal English is intentional and useful when it demonstrates that every Mandarin word was understood.
- Evaluate semantic completeness first; give a polished English rendering separately rather than penalizing literal phrasing.
