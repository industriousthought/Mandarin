# 2026-10-08

## Scope
This summary covers practice from 2026-10-04 through 2026-10-08. No new future vocabulary batch was added. Vocabulary added to the tracker in this update consists only of words/phrases already encountered in practice.

## Strong
- Core sentence parsing and familiar daily-routine vocabulary remain strong.
- Tense/time-reference reasoning improved substantially. The learner now regularly notices when Mandarin leaves English tense ambiguous and correctly uses context to choose past, present, or future.
- 先…再…, 然后, and 以后 are functioning well in multi-clause sequences.
- 不用, 不能, 可以, 所以, 想, 回家, 在家, 休息, 工作, 睡觉, 早饭, and other routine vocabulary are highly reliable.
- The learner now understands that 做 and 做完 differ: 完 explicitly marks completion.
- The learner explicitly noticed that 再做 does not imply finishing, which shows good sensitivity to what is and is not encoded.

## Improving / learning
- 以前 vs 以后: one reversal of 睡觉以前 as "after going to bed"; later examples were correct.
- 完/result complements: initially unknown, now usually correct in 吃完 / 做完 / 看完.
- 做不完 potential complement: first read as simple non-completion, then correctly understood as "cannot finish."
- 什么…都不…: missed on first encounter, then translated correctly in multiple anything/anywhere examples.
- 件 as the measure word for 事情: initially unknown, then quickly learned in numbered-task sentences.
- 还要 and 只能: each had an initial miss and then improved.
- 本来, 留到, 剩下的: understood after explanation but still need spaced review because exposure is limited.

## Shaky / specific weaknesses
- 办公室 was unknown on its only exposure.
- 就 is conceptually understood as "once/then" linking events tightly, but one translation incorrectly supplied "can" when 可以 was not present.
- 到 in 留到 was initially mistaken for a completion complement; keep contrasting 到 (reach/endpoint) with 完 (completion).
- English translations sometimes add a plausible nuance that Mandarin does not explicitly encode, e.g. "can," "finish," or a more specific tense. The learner often catches this independently.

## Grammar status
- Productive/strong: cause-result 所以; 想; basic modal distinction 不用/不能/可以; tense from context; 先…再… / 然后 / 以后.
- Learning: 以前 vs 以后; 完 result complements; potential complements; 还要/只能; 什么…都不…; 件 measure-word pattern; 就; Verb + 得 + description; 太…了; 本来; 留到; 剩下的 nominalization.
- Older adjective + 的 + noun remains under-reviewed and should return later, but not at the expense of the newer active material.

## Next practice
- Do not add another planned vocabulary batch yet.
- Consolidate current vocabulary in varied but familiar sentence structures.
- Mix 以前/以后 and 完/做不完 with older solid material.
- Revisit 就 without 可以 to keep sequencing separate from ability/permission.
- Begin occasional English-to-Mandarin production checks once the current recognition set is stable.
